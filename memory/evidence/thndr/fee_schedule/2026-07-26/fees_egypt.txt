EGYPT / STOCKS & ETFs SECTION ONLY. US and UAE sections omitted (out of scope, R6).

Entity: Thndr Securities Brokerage, licensed by the Financial Regulatory
Authority (FRA) of Egypt, licence no. 804.

--- THNDR BROKERAGE FEES (Stocks and ETFs) ---
Fee                  | Amount                          | Basis
Fixed order fee      | 2 EGP                           | Per order
Variable order fee   | 0.1% of executed order value    | Per order

Note: Thndr Trader subscribers get 50 waived commission orders per month.

--- THIRD-PARTY FEES (passed through at cost) ---
Entity           | Rate    | Basis           | Min   | Max
EGX              | 0.01%   | Per order       | 0 EGP | 5,000 EGP
MCDR             | 0.01%   | Per order       | 0 EGP | 5,000 EGP
FRA              | 0.005%  | Per transaction | 1 EGP | 250 EGP
Risk insurance   | 0.005%  | Per order       | 0 EGP | 5,000 EGP

Note: "An order is what you submit in the app. A transaction is an executed
order — or part of one. A single order can result in multiple transactions if
it fills across different counterparties. The FRA fee applies per transaction,
not per order."

--- WORKED EXAMPLE FROM THE SOURCE ---
Buy order worth 5,000 EGP executing as a single transaction:
  Thndr fixed fee    : 2.00 EGP
  Thndr variable fee : 5.00 EGP   (0.1% x 5,000)
  Total brokerage    : 7.00 EGP
  EGX                : 0.50 EGP   (0.01%)
  MCDR               : 0.50 EGP   (0.01%)
  FRA                : 1.00 EGP   (minimum applies; 0.005% x 5,000 = 0.25)
  Risk insurance     : 0.25 EGP   (0.005%)
  Total third-party  : 2.25 EGP
  TOTAL FEES         : 9.25 EGP

--- MCDR ANNUAL CUSTODY FEE ---
Rate 0.01%, basis: market value of stock portfolio on December 31st.
Deducted from the Thndr Wallet in January. Stock holdings only; mutual funds
excluded. Example given: 75,000 EGP portfolio -> 7.50 EGP.

--- FAQ POINTS RELEVANT TO THE ENGINE ---
"Are fees charged on both buy and sell orders?  Yes — brokerage and
third-party fees apply to both buy and sell orders."
"Third-party government fees cannot be waived — they are set by regulators."

--- MUTUAL FUNDS ---
Thndr charges no fees or commission on mutual fund orders, and there are no
third-party fees. Funds may charge their own subscription/redemption fees set
by the fund administrator.
