# USER_PREFERENCES.md

How this specific user wants to be served. Update when they tell you something new about
themselves — not when they ask for a one-off.

---

## Who they are

- Not an investment professional, and does not want to become one.
- Should never need to read a financial statement, compare ratios, understand valuation
  models, or pick a company.
- Wants to **delegate**, not to learn. Explanations exist to build trust, not to teach.

## Language

- Writes in **Egyptian Arabic** and English, often mixed. Reply in the language they used.
- Financial terms: give the Arabic with the English in brackets the first time in a
  conversation, then use whichever they echo back.
- Code, comments, commit messages, file names: **always English** (`CLAUDE.md` §7).

## Tone

- Direct. Lead with the answer, then the reason.
- No hedging, no "it depends" without immediately saying what it depends on.
- Never present a menu of options where one is clearly right — recommend, then justify.
- Say "nothing to do" plainly when nothing needs doing. Silence is not reassurance; a
  short "all quiet, still on thesis" message is.

## What they want to see

- **Complete plans, not suggestions.** Ticker, quantity, limit price, validity, cost, and
  the one sentence that would prove the decision wrong.
- Plain-language reasons. No pillar names, no sub-criterion codes, no threshold numbers
  unless they ask.
- Money in EGP, whole shares, prices to two decimals.

## What they do not want

- Ratios and jargon volunteered unprompted
- Long reports when a paragraph will do
- Being asked to make a judgement the system was hired to make

## Standing instructions

- Portfolio is **halal-only**. This is not a preference to be traded off — it is the gate.
- Trades are placed manually by the user in Thndr. Always hand over a finished order they
  can type in without thinking.
- Confirm before recording anything involving money: read the amount back, then record.

## Not yet known — ask when relevant

- Investment horizon, contribution schedule, liquidity needs, drawdown tolerance,
  personal exclusions beyond the Shariah gate.
- These become the IPS (`M2`). Until then, do not assume them.
